# Tool rental

We rent a carpet cleaner, a tile saw, a floor sander and a pressure washer. Each rental needs a $50 deposit and a photo ID.

A rental day runs from opening time to opening time the next day. The carpet cleaner costs $35 a day, the tile saw $45, the floor sander $60 and the pressure washer $40.

Late returns are charged a full extra day. The deposit is refunded when the tool comes back clean and working.
