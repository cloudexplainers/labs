# Paint help

One gallon of wall paint covers about 350 square feet with one coat. Most walls need two coats, so plan for 175 square feet per gallon.

To find your wall area, multiply the length of each wall by the ceiling height, add them up, and subtract about 20 square feet for each door and 15 for each window.

We tint paint at the counter while you wait, usually in about ten minutes. Bring a chip or a photo and we can match most colours.
