# Returns

Most items can be returned within 30 days of purchase with the receipt, for a refund to the original payment method.

Power tools that have been opened or used, such as drills, sanders and saws, can be returned within 14 days only if they still work and every part is back in the box. A 15% restocking fee is taken off the refund.

Paint that has been tinted or mixed to a custom colour cannot be returned. Unopened cans of stock colours follow the normal 30 day rule.

Without a receipt we give store credit only, at the lowest price the item sold for in the last 30 days.
