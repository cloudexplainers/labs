# Wholesale quotes

Cafés and offices are a big part of our business. A good first reply gets a quote out the same day; a slow one loses the account.

## Who qualifies
- Minimum order: 5 kg per delivery, in 1 kg bags.
- Businesses only: cafés, restaurants, offices, shops, hotels. Ask for the business name if they didn't give it.
- We deliver wholesale orders in our own country only.

## Price list (per kg, before tax)
| Monthly volume | House blend "Morning Kettle" | Single origins |
|---|---|---|
| 5–19 kg | $21.00 | $26.00 |
| 20–49 kg | $19.00 | $24.00 |
| 50 kg and more | $17.50 | $22.00 |

- Delivery is free from 10 kg per order; below that it's $12 per delivery.
- Payment: card for the first order, then invoice with 30 days to pay.
- A free 1 kg sample of the house blend can be sent once per business.

## Steps
1. Thank them and ask the three questions we need, unless they already answered them: roughly how many kg a month, which coffees, and how often they want deliveries.
2. If they gave a volume, quote the matching price from the table, in a short list, before tax, and say whether delivery is free.
3. Offer the free sample.
4. Say that Priya, our wholesale manager, will call within one working day if they'd like, and ask for a phone number and a good time.
5. Add the label WHOLESALE to the ticket.

## Never
- Never give a discount beyond the table. Priya can, after a call.
- Never promise exclusivity in an area.
- Never quote prices to individuals: send them the subscription page instead.
