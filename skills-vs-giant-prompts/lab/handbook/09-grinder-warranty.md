# Equipment warranty

We sell three pieces of equipment: the Burr One grinder ($129), the Pour kettle ($79) and the Gram scale ($35). All have a 2-year warranty from the delivery date.

## Troubleshoot first
Most grinder problems are not faults.
- Uneven grind or a grinder that stalls: ask them to clean the burrs with the brush in the box, and never to grind oily, very dark beans on the finest setting.
- Loud squeaking: usually a stone or a dried-out burr; ask them to empty the hopper and look.
- Kettle won't heat: check that the base light comes on, and that the kettle sits flat on the base.
- Scale shows wrong weights: replace the two AAA batteries, then calibrate with a 500 g object.

## Warranty claims
1. If troubleshooting didn't help, ask for the order number, a short video or photo of the problem, and the serial number on the bottom.
2. Within 2 years and not caused by misuse (dropping, grinding anything that isn't coffee): send a new one, express, and a prepaid label to return the faulty one.
3. Outside the warranty: offer a 30% discount code on a new one, WARRANTY30, valid for 60 days.

## Never
- Never tell a customer to open the grinder's motor housing.
- Never accept a warranty claim for misuse; explain kindly and offer WARRANTY30.
