# Late orders

Customers write when a parcel is slower than they expected. Most "late" parcels are not lost: they are waiting at a sorting centre or were delivered to a neighbour or a parcel locker.

## How late is late?
Count working days since the order shipped (not since it was placed; we roast to order and ship 1–2 working days after the order).
- Domestic standard: expected in 2–4 working days.
- Domestic express: expected next working day.
- International: expected in 5–10 working days.

An order is **late** when it is past the end of its window. It is **probably lost** when it is 5 or more working days past the end of its window.

## Steps
1. Find the ship date and the service (standard, express or international) in the order details the customer or the ticket gives you.
2. Work out whether the order is on time, late, or probably lost.
3. On time: tell the customer the expected window and that the tracking link in their shipping email updates each time the parcel is scanned. Do not offer anything.
4. Late: apologise once, give the tracking status if you have it, and say we will check again in 2 working days. Ask them to check with neighbours and their building's parcel area.
5. Probably lost: apologise, and offer a free replacement shipped by express, or a full refund including shipping. Let the customer choose. Never ask them to wait longer.

## Codes
- Late by 1–4 working days: no code.
- Late by 5 or more working days: give the code LATE10 (10% off their next order) as well as the replacement or refund.
- Express orders that arrive late: refund the express fee ($9.00) without being asked.

## Never
- Never promise a delivery date. Carriers don't give us one; say "expected" and give a window.
- Never blame the carrier by name in writing.
- Never mark an order as lost before 5 working days past its window, even if the customer is upset.

## Template phrases
- "I'm sorry your beans are taking longer than they should."
- "Your parcel was last scanned at …"
- "If it hasn't arrived by …, write back and we'll send a new bag by express, or refund you in full: whichever you prefer."
