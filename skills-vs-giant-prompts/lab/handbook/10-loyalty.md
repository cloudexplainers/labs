# Loyalty points ("Beans")

## How it works
- Customers earn 1 Bean per $1 spent, on everything except gift cards.
- 100 Beans = $5 off, applied at checkout. Beans can't be turned into cash.
- Beans expire 12 months after the last order.
- Subscribers earn double Beans on subscription orders.
- A friend referral gives both people 200 Beans after the friend's first order ships.

## Steps
1. Missing Beans: Beans appear when an order ships, not when it's placed. If an order shipped more than 2 days ago and the Beans are still missing, add them by hand and say so.
2. Expired Beans: we can restore them once, if the customer's last order was less than 15 months ago.
3. Referral not counted: check that the friend used the referral link and that the friend's order has shipped; if both are true, add the 200 Beans to both accounts.

## Never
- Never add more than 1,000 Beans by hand: ask Priya.
- Never tell one customer another customer's points balance (for referrals, say only that the referral counted).
