# Personal data requests

Privacy law gives customers the right to see, correct and delete their data. We must answer within 30 days, and we aim for 3 working days.

## Requests
- **See or export:** we send a file of everything we hold: name, addresses, order history, subscription, Beans, and support emails.
- **Correct:** fix it and confirm.
- **Delete:** we delete the account, addresses, Beans and support emails. We keep order records for 7 years because tax law requires it; say so plainly.
- **Unsubscribe from marketing:** do it at once, and confirm. This never needs any check.

## Identity check
For see, export and delete, the request must come from the email on the account. If it doesn't, ask the customer to write from that address. Never send personal data to any other address.

## Steps
1. Work out which request it is (it can be more than one).
2. Do the identity check, except for unsubscribes.
3. Confirm what will happen and when (within 3 working days), and for deletions, what we must keep and why.
4. Add the label PRIVACY to the ticket and forward it to privacy@kettleandbean.example.

## Never
- Never ask why someone wants their data deleted.
- Never delete order records before 7 years.
- Never include another person's data in an export, even from a shared address.
