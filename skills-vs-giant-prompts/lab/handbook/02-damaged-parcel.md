# Damaged on arrival

Coffee bags can tear, and grinders and mugs can break in transit. Our carrier insurance covers it, so the customer never pays for damage, and they never need to send anything back.

## What counts
- A torn or split bag, beans spilled in the box.
- A bag whose valve or zip is broken.
- A crushed box with damage to what's inside (a crushed box with an intact bag is not damage: thank them for telling us and log it).
- Broken mugs, a cracked grinder hopper, a dented kettle.

## Steps
1. Thank the customer and apologise once.
2. Ask for one photo of the damage and one of the outside of the box. We need both for the carrier claim. If they already sent photos, say thank you and do not ask again.
3. Offer a free replacement of the damaged item, shipped by express at our cost, as soon as we have the photos. If the item is out of stock, offer a full refund of that item instead.
4. Tell them to keep or compost the torn bag's beans if they are clean: don't ask them to return anything.
5. Add the label DAMAGE to the ticket so the warehouse can see if one carrier or one packing station has a pattern.

## Amounts
- Replacement: the same item and size, express shipping free.
- If they would rather have money back: refund the item's price; shipping is refunded only if the whole order was damaged.
- Damage to a grinder or kettle over $80: also ask for the order number on the box label, for the insurance claim.

## Never
- Never ask the customer to pay return postage.
- Never ask for more than two photos.
- Never send a replacement before photos arrive, unless the customer has ordered from us 3 or more times before (then send it straight away and ask for the photos afterwards).

## Template phrases
- "I'm so sorry your bag arrived torn. That's not how we want your beans to reach you."
- "Could you send one photo of the bag and one of the box? As soon as we have them, a new bag goes out by express, at no cost to you."
