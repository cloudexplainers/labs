# Allergens and ingredients

Our coffee is just roasted coffee beans, but customers with allergies need exact answers, and a wrong one can make someone ill. Be precise and never guess.

## What we know for certain
- All our coffees are 100% arabica beans, nothing added. No flavourings, no oils, no sugar.
- Our roastery also packs hazelnut and almond biscotti on a separate line in the same building. We cannot promise that any product is free from traces of nuts.
- We do not handle gluten-containing grains except in the biscotti, which contain wheat flour. Coffee is packed on a different line, but in the same room.
- No dairy is used anywhere in the building.
- Decaf: our only decaf (Colombia "Sugarcane") is decaffeinated with ethyl acetate from sugarcane, not with methylene chloride. It is 99.9% caffeine-free by weight, so a cup still has about 2–5 mg of caffeine.
- A regular cup of our filter coffee has roughly 80–120 mg of caffeine; espresso roughly 60–80 mg per shot. These are typical ranges, not measurements of our beans.

## Steps
1. Answer the exact question asked, using only the facts above.
2. For any nut allergy question, always say plainly that we cannot guarantee our coffee is free from traces of nuts, because nuts are handled in the same building.
3. For medical questions (pregnancy, heart conditions, medication), give the facts above and suggest they check with their doctor. Never give medical advice.
4. If the question is about a product we don't sell, say so; don't guess about other brands.

## Never
- Never say "nut-free", "allergen-free" or "safe for allergies".
- Never estimate caffeine for a specific medical situation.
- Never promise that a new product will be allergen-free before it has a label.

## Template phrases
- "Our coffee is just roasted arabica beans, with nothing added."
- "Because we pack biscotti with hazelnuts and almonds in the same building, we can't guarantee our coffee is free from traces of nuts."
