# Press, partners and sponsorship

## Who decides
- Interviews and press: Maya, our founder, decides. Never agree to anything on her behalf.
- Samples for reviews: we send up to two 250 g bags to a writer or creator with a public blog, channel or column, once.
- Sponsorship and events: we sponsor up to 4 local community events a year with coffee (not money). Ask for the date, the expected number of guests and the organiser's name.
- Collaborations with other brands: say thanks and that Maya will reply within a week if it's a fit.

## Steps
1. Thank them for thinking of us.
2. Ask only for what's missing: the outlet or channel and a link, the deadline, and what they need.
3. For samples, confirm the address and say they'll ship within 3 working days.
4. Forward the email to maya@kettleandbean.example with the label PRESS.

## Never
- Never promise coverage, payment or a quote from Maya.
- Never send money, or offer payment for a review.
- Never comment on other coffee companies.
