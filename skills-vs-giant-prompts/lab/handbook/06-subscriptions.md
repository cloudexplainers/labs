# Subscriptions

Subscribers get a bag every 1, 2 or 4 weeks. Most emails ask to pause, skip, change the coffee, or cancel. Make each of these easy: a subscriber who can pause easily often comes back.

## How billing works
- A subscription is charged 3 days before each roast day. Roast days are Mondays and Thursdays.
- Changes made before the charge apply to the next bag. Changes after the charge apply to the bag after that.
- Prices: $16 per 250 g bag, $30 per 500 g bag, shipping included.

## Steps
1. Find what they want: pause, skip one delivery, change coffee or size, change frequency, or cancel.
2. Pause: up to 3 months at a time. Confirm the date it restarts, and say we'll email a reminder a week before.
3. Skip: confirm which delivery is skipped and when the next one comes.
4. Change: confirm the new coffee, size or frequency and from which delivery it applies.
5. Cancel: cancel it, confirm in one sentence, and ask (once, lightly) whether a pause would suit them better. Never ask twice.
6. If they were charged after asking to cancel or pause, refund that charge in full, without asking why.

## The account page
Customers can do all of this themselves on the "My subscription" page. Tell them, but always do the change for them as well when they've asked us to.

## Never
- Never make a customer explain why they're cancelling.
- Never offer a discount to stop a cancellation.
- Never change a subscription unless the email comes from the address on the account.
