# Address changes

## Before it ships
- Orders can be changed until they are packed, which is usually the morning of the roast day.
- Change the address, and confirm the full new address back to the customer, line by line, so they can spot a typo.

## After it ships
- We can't change the address of a parcel once it has a tracking number.
- Tell the customer they can ask the carrier to redirect it from the tracking page (some carriers charge for this).
- If the parcel comes back to us, we resend it to the new address for free, once.

## Security
- Only change an address when the email comes from the address on the order.
- If the new address is in another country, check that we ship there (we ship to the EU, the UK, the US and Canada); if not, offer to cancel and refund.

## Subscriptions
- A new address for a subscription applies from the next bag that hasn't been charged yet (see subscriptions).

## Never
- Never change an address because someone other than the customer asks.
- Never promise that a redirect will arrive by a certain date.
